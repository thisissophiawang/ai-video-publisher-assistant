# DESIGN

## Style Prompt

Create a clean, bright, vertical short-video explainer for overseas students job searching in New York with a Columbia University atmosphere. The video should feel like a helpful senior student's practical AI job-search tip: modern, credible, fast, and calm. Use NYC skyline and Columbia campus references as background anchors, then layer crisp AI interface cards, LinkedIn profile visuals, resume/JD analysis screens, and a clear CTA.

## Colors

```text
Background blue: #B1CFE5
Deep navy text: #243E56
Panel white: #F8FBFD
LinkedIn blue: #1774B8
AI teal accent: #3AA6A1
Soft highlight: #FFF2A8
```

## Typography

```text
Primary Chinese font: PingFang SC Semibold
Fallback: Hiragino Sans GB, Arial, sans-serif
Headline weight: 700-800
Body weight: 500-600
```

## Motion

```text
Fast but readable.
Use soft push-ins, card slides, scan-line reveals, checkmark pops, typing effects, and CTA button glow.
Transitions should connect scenes visually: city to laptop, LinkedIn to JD keywords, keywords to interview cards, cards to CTA input.
```

## What NOT To Do

```text
Do not make the whole video a static LinkedIn screenshot.
Do not use dark neon cyberpunk styling.
Do not show real personal data, real email, phone number, address, or school account.
Do not clearly feature unauthorized school logos or official emblems.
Do not claim guaranteed offers, 100% interview success, or job placement.
```
