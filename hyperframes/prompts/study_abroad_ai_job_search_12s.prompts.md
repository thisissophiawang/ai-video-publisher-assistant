# Prompts: Study Abroad AI Job Search 12s

## Style Prompt

```text
Clean modern tech-forward vertical short video, overseas student job search scene in New York City, Columbia University atmosphere, NYC skyline, campus study space, bright natural lighting, white light-gray blue green palette, laptop screen with AI assistant interface, professional but approachable, knowledge tutorial style, no real personal information, no unauthorized logos.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No real email addresses, no phone numbers, no home addresses, no school account pages, no guaranteed job offer claims, no unauthorized brand logos, no clear school logos or official emblems, no distorted text, no crowded messy interface, no dark horror mood, no fake offer letter.
```

## Shot Prompts

### Shot 1

```text
Vertical 9:16 video shot of an overseas student in New York, Columbia University campus or library atmosphere, sitting at a desk with a laptop, browsing English job descriptions and resume documents, NYC skyline or campus details in the background, slight stress but focused, clean modern style, bright soft lighting, safe lower area for Chinese subtitles.
```

### Shot 2

```text
Vertical 9:16 fast montage, AI assistant interface analyzing an English job description for a New York job, extracting role keywords, improving an English resume, generating a Cover Letter outline, mock interview question cards, subtle Columbia University and NYC visual atmosphere, clean blue and green highlights, no real company logos or private data.
```

### Shot 3

```text
Vertical 9:16 shot of a tidy job application tracker dashboard with statuses like Applied, Interview, Follow-up, then an overseas student confidently preparing for an online interview with a soft NYC skyline background, modern clean tech style, safe area for subtitles and call to action.
```
