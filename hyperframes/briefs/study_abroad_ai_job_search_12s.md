# Video Brief: 海外留学生如何用 AI 提升求职效率

## 1. Delivery Specs

- Topic: 海外留学生怎么用 AI 提升求职效率
- Format: MP4
- Resolution: 720 x 1280
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Duration: 12 seconds
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Voiceover language: Chinese
- Subtitle language: Chinese
- Platforms: 抖音 / 视频号 / 小红书

## 2. Content Structure

### 0-3s: Pain Hook

Goal: Immediately show the job-search pressure of overseas students.

Visual direction:

- Overseas student browsing English job posts on a laptop.
- Screen shows resume files, job descriptions, and email windows.

Voiceover/subtitle:

```text
海外留学生找工作，别再一个个手动改简历了。
```

### 3-8s: AI Efficiency Methods

Goal: Show specific job-search tasks AI can speed up.

Visual direction:

- AI analyzing an English job description.
- AI extracting role keywords.
- AI improving an English resume.
- AI generating a Cover Letter or interview questions.

Voiceover/subtitle:

```text
用 AI 分析 JD、优化简历关键词、生成 Cover Letter，还能模拟英文面试。
```

### 8-12s: Result And CTA

Goal: Show the benefit and guide users to follow.

Visual direction:

- Application tracker shows multiple job statuses.
- Student confidently preparing for an interview.

Voiceover/subtitle:

```text
把重复工作交给 AI，你把时间留给面试准备。关注我，下一期教你搭 AI 求职流程。
```

## 3. Visual Rules

- Use a clean, modern, tech-forward visual style.
- Keep all shots in 9:16 vertical composition.
- Place the main subject or laptop screen slightly above center.
- Put subtitles in the lower safe area.
- Do not cover faces, resume titles, buttons, or important screen content.
- Suggested colors: white, light gray, blue, and green.
- Highlight keywords with no more than 2 accent colors.
- Use 3-5 shots total.
- Keep each shot around 2-4 seconds.
- The first 3 seconds must show the pain point or key benefit.
- Show concrete AI use cases before the 8-second mark.
- The final 2 seconds must include a CTA.

## 4. Safety And Compliance

- Do not show real personal private information.
- Do not show real school accounts, emails, phone numbers, or addresses.
- Do not promise "guaranteed offer" or "100% job placement".
- Do not use unauthorized logos, portraits, or music.
- Do not fake admission, hiring, or platform results.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

- Final output is an MP4 file.
- Resolution is 720 x 1280.
- Duration is 12 seconds, with tolerance no more than 0.5 seconds.
- Video is vertical 9:16.
- Video includes visuals, voiceover, subtitles, and background music.
- File plays normally with no black screen, glitching, missing audio, or stutter.
- Topic is clearly about overseas students using AI to improve job-search efficiency.
- The first 3 seconds clearly communicate the pain point.
- The middle section shows at least 3 AI job-search uses.
- The ending includes a follow, save, or next-episode CTA.
- Important visual information stays out of platform UI cover zones.
